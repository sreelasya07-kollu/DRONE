# RESQ-OPS Emergency Response Knowledge Base

## Initial Response

1. Confirm the incident location.
2. Identify victims and hazards.
3. Prioritize victims according to severity and number of people affected.
4. Identify available response assets.
5. Calculate a safe route.
6. Dispatch the appropriate response unit.
7. Monitor the mission in real time.
8. Update mission status continuously.

## Victim Prioritization

Critical cases should receive immediate attention.

Priority can consider:

- Medical severity
- Number of people
- Vulnerability
- Waiting time
- Nearby hazards

## Drone Operations

Before dispatching a drone:

- Check battery.
- Check mission distance.
- Check current status.
- Verify that sufficient reserve remains.
- Avoid hazardous areas where possible.

## Hazard Management

Hazards should be represented on the operational map.

Examples:

- Flooded roads
- Fire
- Debris
- Chemical hazards
- Blocked roads
- Collapsed structures

## Route Planning

Routes should consider:

- Distance
- Hazards
- Accessibility
- Estimated travel time

## AI Safety

AI detections are decision-support information.

Critical detections should be verified by an operator before irreversible action.
